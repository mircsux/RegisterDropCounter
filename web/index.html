About

Same integer-cent drop math as the Windows, Android, iPhone, and C23 builds. Designed by Ronald Robbins Jr and SuperGrok.

Yellow cells are the only inputs. Drop order: $100, $50, $20, $10, $5, $2, $1, quarters, dimes, nickels, rolls, pennies. Left turns green when it equals the register base.

Counts and history stay in this browser (localStorage). Copy buttons write tab-separated cash-log rows.
