Register Drop Counter  2.30.0  (2026-09-19)  -  iPhone (Xcode)

1. Unzip this folder on a Mac.
2. Open RegisterDropCounter.xcodeproj in Xcode 15 or newer.
3. Plug in your iPhone and select it as the run destination.
4. Signing: select the RegisterDropCounter target, Team = your Apple ID
   (free account is enough to run on your own phone).
5. Press Run. Trust the developer on the iPhone if iOS asks
   (Settings > General > VPN & Device Management).

The app is Drop Counter on the home screen. Same drop math as the
Windows and web versions: yellow counts, $2 bills, undo, drop slip,
named tills, history.

You can also add the web counter to an iPhone home screen from Safari:
Share > Add to Home Screen.
